## Welcome to VinUniversity Admissions

Admissions at VinUniversity is designed with our student – you – in the mind. That is why our process is simple, thoughtful, and grants you a lot of space for self-expression. Our goal is to educate and train outstanding talents; therefore, the university is committed to provide generous scholarships and financial aid to deserving talented students.  
 We are not a university of the privileged, but indeed a university of talents. Welcome to the first step in demonstrating your talent and realizing the commitment you bring to our community, Vietnam, and the world!

###

Interested students can apply to VinUni using the online application form available [**here**](https://vinuni.force.com/s/login/). The application form has several sections. Applicants must complete all sections and provide all the information listed below before they can apply. All applications must be submitted online. No applications or supporting documents can be submitted on paper, or via email. The information requested in the application is listed below:

* Personal Information
* Academic Achievements: GPA, A-Level exam, IB results, Olympics awards, competitions, etc. over the last 3 years
* Standardized test results from SAT/ACT/Subject Tests if you have taken any such tests
* English proficiency certificate with validity (IELTS, TOEFL) if you have taken any such tests
* One 400 word essay
* Non-academic achievements (competitions, sports, music, arts, volunteer activity, and anything else that is of interest to you)
* Reference contacts from teachers who know you well

Required Documents (msut be uploaded to the online application before submitting):

* Copy of your National ID or passport
* 1-page CV (using the template provided in the application form)
* Copies of transcripts from Grades 9, 10, 11 and 12 (if available)
* Optional supporting documents: declaration of self-work, copies of certificates, transcripts to support the academic and non-academic achievements.

###

The Early Admission cycle is for those applicants who have a keen desire to attend VinUni, and would like to have an assurance of getting into VinUni at the earliest opportunity.

All applicants who meet the minimum eligibility threshold but are not successful in the Early Admission cycle will be automatically added to the pool of applicants in the Regular Admission cycle.

The Regular Admission cycle will involve the same review process as Early Admission.
