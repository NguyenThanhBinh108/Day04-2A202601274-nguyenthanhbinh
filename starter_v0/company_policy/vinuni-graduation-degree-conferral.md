## 1. Purpose

This procedure standardizes the steps in the process of graduation review and degree conferral for students, ensuring consistency and quality in the degree-granting process and establishing a legal framework for diploma management and issuance according to regulations.

## 2. Scope and Application

- Scope: This procedure applies to full-time undergraduate students who have completed their academic program and meet graduation requirements; Colleges; Office of University Registrar, Graduation Review Board Committee.
- Application: This procedure applies to the entire process of graduation review and degree conferral for all full-time undergraduate programs at VinUni.

## 3. Legal Basis

- Decision No. 1824/QD-TTg dated December 17, 2019, by the Prime Minister regarding the establishment of VinUniversity;
- Higher Education Law dated June 18, 2012; Amendments to the Higher Education Law dated November 19, 2018, and Decree 99/2019/ND-CP dated December 30, 2019;
- Circular No. 08/2021/TT-BGDDT dated March 18, 2021, by the Ministry of Education and Training on Undergraduate Education Regulations;
- Circular No. 21/2019/TT-BGDDT dated November 29, 2019, by the Ministry of Education and Training on Management of Academic Credentials;
- VinUniversity’s Academic Credentials Management Regulations effective July 01, 2023;
- VinUniversity’s current Academic Regulations for Full-time Undergraduates.
