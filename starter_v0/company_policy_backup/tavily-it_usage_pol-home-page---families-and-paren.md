## Welcome to the VinUniversity Family

At VinUni, we are dedicated to fostering strong connections between the University and students’ families, believing that these partnerships are key to supporting your student’s growth and success. This is why we created the VinUni Family chapter, managed by the [VinUni Community](https://www.facebook.com/groups/vinunicommunity/) where students, parents, faculty, and alumni united in a vibrant network committed to the innovation and excellence of our University while creating new values for society.
