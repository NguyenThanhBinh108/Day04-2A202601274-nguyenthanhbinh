# Applying for Ethical Review

Step-by-step guide to IRB approval for research involving human participants at VinUniversity.

IRBGuide

## Prepare Your Required Documents

📋

### IRB Application Form

* **Shortened form** — minimal risk studies
* **Full form** — moderate / high risk(s)
* Submit MS Word + signed & sealed scanned version

[⬇ Shortened Form](https://research.vinuni.edu.vn/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Expedited-Review-Protocol-form-for-VinUniversity.docx) [⬇ Full Form](https://research.vinuni.edu.vn/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/ERC-CHECKLIST-FOR-FULL-REVIEW.docx)

📄

### Consent Form

* Use Short or Long Consent Template
* Translate into Vietnamese if participants are Vietnamese
* Custom forms must cover all points in the VinUni template

[⬇ Short Template](https://research.vinuni.edu.vn/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Template-Short-Consent.docx) [⬇ Long Template](https://research.vinuni.edu.vn/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Template-Long-Consent.doc)

### Verified CVs

* Verified CVs by authority

🗂️

### Project-Specific Documents

* Research proposals
* Questionnaire / survey
* Any other study-specific materials

## Submission & Review Process

📧

### Submit to RMO

Send all soft-copy documents to RMO.

[[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#56243325333724353e16203f3823383f78333223782038)

🔍

### RMO Preliminary Review

RMO verifies completeness of submission.

🗓️

### Forward to ERC

RMO forwards application to VinMec–VinUni ERC.

⚖️

### ERC Evaluation

VinMec–VinUni ERC reviews the study.

✅

### Final Approval Letter

Stamped & signed letter issued by ERC Chair.

## Understanding Your Approval

✅

### Valid Approval

Only a stamped & signed letter from the ERC Chair is accepted. Research is subject to regular monitoring for continued compliance with approved protocols.

🚫

### No Retroactive Approval

Before final ERC approval, contacting participants, collecting or analyzing data are unapproved and cannot be used in the study.

5

## Requesting an Amendment

✏️

### Modifying an Approved Study?

Fill out the Amendment Approval form and Details of Changes form. Submit all documents to RMO before implementing any protocol changes.

[⬇ Amendment Approval Form](https://research.vinuni.edu.vn/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Request-for-Amendment-Approval.docx) [⬇ Details of Changes Form](https://research.vinuni.edu.vn/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Details-of-Changes.docx)

## IRB Requirements for External Partners

### Working with External Partners

🤝

Partners are **encouraged to follow the VinUni IRB review process**

📨

If partner has existing IRB approval, **submit approval letter & documents to RMO**

RMO consults VinMec–VinUni IRB to **determine if internal review is still required**
