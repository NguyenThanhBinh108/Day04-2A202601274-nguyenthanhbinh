### VINUNI ANNOUNCES ATTRACTIVE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM FOR ACADEMIC YEAR 2026–2027

#### VINGROUP COMMITS TO PROVIDING TUITION SUPPORT FOR 100% OF VINUNI STUDENTS

To further support the mission of nurturing talent, Vingroup, the Founder of VinUniversity, has reaffirmed its unwavering commitment to sustaining the University’s Scholarship Fund for future generations of VinUni students.

###### Tuition Subsidy: From 2025 to 2030, all students enrolling at VinUniversity will be granted a 35% tuition subsidy, applied for the full duration of their studies.

##### Merit-based Scholarships: Awarded to applicants who demonstrate Aspiration, Intellect, and Resilience. Scholarships range from 50% to 100% of tuition.

* President’s Excellence Scholarship: Covers 100% of tuition and living expenses.
* Provost’s Merit Scholarship: Covers 100% of tuition.
* Dean’s Distinction Scholarship: Covers 80% or 90% of tuition.
* Discipline’s Honor Scholarship: Covers 50%, 60%, or 70% of tuition.

##### Special Scholarships (stackable): These scholarships can be combined with other merit-based awards:

* Special Academic Scholarship: Provides an additional 5% tuition support for Bachelor’s programs.
* WIT (Women in Tech) Scholarship: Provides an additional 5% tuition support for female applicants in technology and science fields.
* Dean Choi Grant by Soosan: Covers 10% of tuition. This grant is generously funded by Soosan Group, awarded as an add-on (stackable) scholarship to further support talented students in their academic journey, based on the selection criteria set forth by the donor.
* Exclusive Scholarship for Filipino Talents (Rolling Basis): Provides up to a full-ride scholarship, covering 100% of tuition fees plus USD 1,500 in living expenses.
* Special Scholarship Privileges for Vingroup Employees.

##### **FINANCIAL AID**

Applicants who meet admission requirements but face financial difficulties may apply for additional Financial Aid, on top of scholarships and sponsorships already received. Financial aid can cover up to 100% of tuition fees.

Scholarships are awarded based on the Admissions Committee’s evaluation criteria and are maintained throughout the program duration, provided that the student meets VinUni’s scholarship retention requirements.

With aspiration, intellect, and resilience, you can become a VinUnian! Apply early to secure your scholarship opportunity!

##### Important admission milestones:

– **Early Admission Round:** 15/10/2025 – 15/01/2026

– **Regular Admission Round:** 15/02/2026 – 15/05/2026

– **Rolling Round:** 15/06/2026 – 15/08/2026

##### Exclusive Benefit for Early Round Applicants:

“Privilege” for candidates applying in the Early Admission round: Waiver of the 2,000,000 VND application fee for candidates who attend VinUni Open Day and other recruitment events organized by the University (further details will be provided in due course). Typically, candidates applying to VinUni, upon passing the document review stage and being invited for an interview, will be required to pay this application fee.
