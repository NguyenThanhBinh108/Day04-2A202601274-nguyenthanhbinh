UNDERGRADUATE STUDENT GUIDE Academic Success, Experience, Campus Life, Career AY24-25 Dear VinUniversity Student, I am truly excited to welcome you to the VinUniversity family. You have persisted through all the challenges to find yourself here where you dreamed to be. You deserve congratulations and so do the wonderful people who have invested their time and efforts in you. I hope you take some time to thank those who made it possible for you to begin your journey at VinUniversity. This Guide is intended as a practical help for you as you start your life at VinUniversity. This Guide covers important topics such as academic requirements, first-year experience, residential life, student activities, and many more. This Guide will help you to be aware of and understand essential policies, values, and standards that you should follow as a valued community member. Here you find the community's expectations of you in terms of honesty, personal conduct, and commitment to a safe living and learning environment. This Guide is available online and will be updated regularly with input from the VinUniversity community.
Therefore., I urge you to take a few minutes to read through this document and feel free to research and dig deeper into those areas of the university that spark your interest.
This year will be a defining time for you in your life. It will be full of opportunities, challenges, and fun. While it will be defined by academic rigor and high expectations, it is important that you allow yourself to explore the campus, try something new, open your mind to new ideas, participate in social activities, and reach out and connect with new people with different views and backgrounds. The choices you make along the way will be crucial to your development, not just as a competent and skilled college graduate, but also as an informed, empathetic, involved, and responsible citizen of the world. VinUniversity staff and faculty are here to help you on your journey. Don't hesitate to reach out to your advisors, faculty, staff, and people you can trust to talk about your academic or personal matters. You are here at VinUniversity because of your talent, your potential, and motivation to make a positive difference in the world. Under the nurturing care of VinUniversity, you will grow and develop your talents to become the future leader that Vietnam and the world needs. I look forward to being part of your journey and witnessing you grow and thrive through your years at VinUni. With best wishes, Prof. K. David Harrison, Vice Provost and Dean of Student Experience Welcome to VinUniversity!
